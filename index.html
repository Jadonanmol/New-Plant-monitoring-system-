Capacitive Soil Moisture v1.2 / v2.0

Analog

Resists soil corrosion by using capacitive frequency sensing rather than exposed metal electrodes.

VCC → ESP32 3.3V (or 5V if 3.3V regulator present)
GND → ESP32 GND (Common Ground)
AOUT → GPIO 34 (ADC1_CH6, Input Only)

DHT22 (AM2302) Temp & Humidity

Digital 1-Wire

High accuracy temperature (±0.5°C) and relative humidity (±2% RH).

VCC → ESP32 3.3V or 5V
DATA → GPIO 4 (with 10kΩ pull-up to VCC)
GND → ESP32 GND

BH1750 Ambient Light Sensor

I2C Digital

Calibrated illuminance measurement in Lux (1 - 65,535 lx).

VCC → ESP32 3.3V
GND → ESP32 GND
SCL → GPIO 22 (Default I2C SCL)
SDA → GPIO 21 (Default I2C SDA)

HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Water Depth

Pulse Timing

Measures distance to water surface inside reservoir tank.

VCC → External 5V (or VIN)
TRIG → GPIO 12 (Output trigger)
ECHO → GPIO 14 (via 1k/2k voltage divider to 3.3V!)
GND → ESP32 GND

4-Channel 5V Optocoupler Relay

Relay Control

Controls 12V/5V DC water pump, grow lights, fan, and humidifier.

VCC → External 5V (Remove JD-VCC jumper)
IN1 (Pump) → GPIO 26
IN2 (Fan) → GPIO 27
IN3 (Lights) → GPIO 25 / IN4 → GPIO 33

Power Supply & Flyback Protection

Electrical Safety

Never power the water pump directly from the ESP32 3.3V or 5V rail!

1. Use external 5V/12V 2A DC adapter for pump & relays.
2. Connect all GND lines together (Common Ground).
3. Place a 1N4007 flyback diode across inductive pump motor.
